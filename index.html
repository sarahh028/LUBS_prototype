About the event

  • We are a small Community Benefit Society whose mission is to create community-owned clean energy solutions that reduce carbon emissions, lower energy costs and support a more sustainable future for Leeds.
  • Join us for a fancy-dress 5k at Woodhouse Moor, followed by coffee, cake and a chance to meet new people!
  • Bring your friends or come along on your own, put on your most creative costume and enjoy a morning of running and conversation.
  • After the run, meet the Bright Power Leeds team and discover how you could get involved in community-owned clean energy.
  • Whether you are interested in sustainability, looking to gain volunteering experience or simply curious about what we do, this is a relaxed opportunity to find out more!
